Status grades the instrument, not the outcome. Substantially delivered means the thing the mission promised to create exists — a scheme notified, a rule amended, a forum launched. It does not mean the associated output target has been met, which is why a delivered sub-component can sit beside an empty progress bar. Announced, not notified marks instruments that exist in a minister's statement or a cabinet note but have no published scheme or guideline. No public record means nothing has been published against a written commitment; every such row carries the scope of what was searched and as of when, because absence of disclosure is not proof of inaction.

Numbers carry their own state. A filled bar is a figure verified against a government source. An outlined empty bar is a confirmed none — the thing does not exist. A hatched bar is unreported, where no figure has been published either way, and it is never drawn as zero. A greyed bar marked not comparable means the figure and the target do not share a population or a counting rule, so the share shown is context rather than delivery. Each metric carries an as-of date, and Counting rules in the header reveals how every headline figure was derived. Where two government sources conflict, both are shown rather than one silently chosen.

Primary sources are the NCMM mission document (January 2025) and the Ministry of Mines progress deck of 10 January 2026 and the Ministry of Mines Annual Report 2025-26, with the PIB NCMM factsheet of 18 August 2026, updated 23 October 2026 taking precedence where the three conflict. Supplemented where no government disclosure exists. Government sources are outlined in the accent colour.
